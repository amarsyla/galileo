# Galileo

**Turn a phone into a science lab.** Film a swinging pendulum in slow motion, and Galileo tracks it, draws the physics right on top of your video, and helps you discover the rule behind it, all the way to measuring gravity yourself.

Built for students (roughly ages 12 to 16) and their teachers, especially in schools with no lab equipment. A phone, some string, and a printed marker are enough.

Built with Meta models: SAM 3.1, Muse Spark, Muse Spark Open, and Muse Image.

## Status
